# Astral Music — Brand identity

## Official identity
- Product: Astral Music
- Creator: Astral Studio
- Slogan: Tu música, Tus Gustos.
- Visual direction: Glass / Liquid Glass
- Primary mark: the silver orbital loop and four-point star supplied by the creator.

## Visual system
- Canvas: Space Black — #000000
- Primary accent: Astral Violet — #8B72D8
- Deep accent: #49366F
- Metallic highlight: Astral Silver — #D8DDE8
- Glass glint: Astral Glow — #66D9DFFF
- Typography: existing Pinyon Script, EB Garamond, Montserrat and JetBrains Mono type families.

## Logo use
- Keep the orbital emblem and four-point star together.
- Prefer a black background and preserve the silver/white metallic treatment.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, or place busy artwork behind the mark.
- Keep the complete mark within the Android adaptive-icon safe area.
- At small sizes, use the emblem without extra wording.

## Product voice
Premium, calm, personal, and music-first. Astral should feel distinctive without compromising the existing playback experience.

## Compatibility
The existing application ID and signing configuration are intentionally unchanged so signed releases can remain eligible to update existing installations.
